## Scheduling and recovery boundary

The first systemd-supervised pass completed 16 protocol plans serially. The
continuation excluded those plan IDs and used the previously validated safety
lanes:

- independent protocol groups: two workers;
- four CIBA groups: one worker, preserving the shared applicant/session/decision boundary;
- logout/session browser groups: two isolated suite worktrees;
- OpenID4VC: all 17 plans in one group, overlapping only after protocol onboarding;
- protocol and OpenID4VC: separate official-suite worktrees and separate conformance leases.

Starting both administrative onboarding flows at the same instant caused the
second flow's TOTP to be rejected as a replay. The durable local coordinator at
`caeaa9898873f30a023c73e5b019747faad3a395` now waits for an atomic protocol
readiness marker before starting OpenID4VC. This follow-up was verified by 371
unit tests (7 skipped) and the static contract gate; the successful official
result above used the equivalent corrected operational ordering.

The public TLS endpoint uses an RSA certificate and the two permitted
ECDHE-RSA AES-GCM TLS 1.2 cipher suites. This follows FAPI 1.0 Advanced section
8.5 and RFC 9325 section 4.2; ECDHE-ECDSA and static-RSA TLS 1.2 handshakes were
rejected before the resumed matrix.
